Foster Care

Open your home with training, guidance, and ongoing support.

Guardian Light walks with foster parents from the first conversation through licensing, placement, and the day-to-day care children need to heal and thrive.

Ask About Foster CareFamily Support Services

What to expect

A clear path from interest to placement.

  • Initial conversation to answer questions and talk through readiness
  • Training that prepares families for real foster care needs
  • Home study and licensing support
  • Placement matching and ongoing specialist support
  • Continued education and connection with Guardian Light staff

Foster parent support

Children need safe, compassionate caregivers. Foster parents need an agency that stays available after approval.

Preparation

Learn the foster care process, expectations, and practical caregiving tools before a child enters your home.

Partnership

Work with foster care specialists who help coordinate care, documentation, meetings, and next steps.

Ongoing care

Receive continuing support as needs change, questions come up, and families move through each stage of care.
