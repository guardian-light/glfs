Family Support

Support for families building safer, steadier routines.

Guardian Light helps families practice new skills, strengthen communication, navigate visits, and create practical plans that support child safety and family stability.

Request InformationSee Preservation Services

What family support can include

  • Supervised visitation and visitation coaching
  • Parenting classes and in-home parenting support
  • Drug testing coordination when ordered or referred
  • Skill building, mentoring, and accountability
  • Coordination with DHHS, courts, and referral sources

How the work feels

Family support is hands-on and practical. Workers help parents add tools to their toolbox, practice routines between visits, and move forward without judgment.

Services can be coordinated alongside other Guardian Light programs when a family needs more than one kind of support.

Next steps

If you are a family, caseworker, attorney, or referral source, contact Guardian Light to discuss availability in your area.

Start the conversation

Call or email with the service need, county, timeline, and referral details.

Build a plan

The team confirms the service type, goals, schedule, and coordination needs.

Begin support

Families receive consistent guidance, documentation, and practical coaching.
